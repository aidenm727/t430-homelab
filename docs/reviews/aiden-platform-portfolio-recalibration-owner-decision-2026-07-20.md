At the pinned baseline:

- the engineering foundation, repository knowledge, validation, synchronization, infrastructure, and operational controls are genuine strengths;
- the portfolio contains substantially more reviewed design than released integrated capability;
- EO-2026-013 has substantial implementation through B2a but no complete end-to-end context compiler;

- EO-2026-022 remains captured rather than implemented;

## 4. Accepted Strategic Conclusions

The owner accepts these conclusions:

1. The platform's primary bottleneck has shifted from missing architecture to release conversion, human operability, and direct repeated use.
2. Engineering rigor remains a major asset and should be preserved.
3. Future foundation work must demonstrate a short path to an actual consumer.
4. The next 90-day sequence is engineering-system v1.0, a useful school capability, an initial career workflow, a deployed hub or control surface, and reduced ceremony.
5. EO-2026-013 may continue only through a bounded minimum functional release.
6. A school capability and EO-2026-022 are the next major outcome tracks.
7. Atlas should remain deterministic and repository-oriented while being evaluated through real consumer value and better human visibility.
8. Portfolio consolidation and relationship cleanup are needed, but no merge, retirement, or lifecycle change is currently authorized.
9. Evidence and approval depth should remain proportionate to risk.
10. The HTML dashboard and spreadsheet are useful derived views but are not canonical sources.

These warnings shape stop conditions and sequencing. They do not automatically authorize the challenger's proposed implementation.

## 8. B2b Hard Kill Switch

B2b must stop and return for owner review when:

- any schema or policy change is required;
- any accepted B1 or B2a behavior must change;
- a new dependency, Atlas command, provider adapter, database, network capability, or protected-content access is proposed;
- any code path outside the exact seven-path boundary is required;
- the compiler cannot remain a pure in-memory library boundary;
- implementation exceeds four focused engineering sessions or approximately twelve owner-hours;
- complete package validation requires a committed golden fixture;
- no concrete next consumer can be named;
- the work begins decomposing into another extended checkpoint sequence.

The time estimate is a control boundary, not measured telemetry.
